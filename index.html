01 — Vision

The idea in full

A national points system — like airline miles, but for being Tanzanian — where youth on both sides of the Union earn rewards by doing things that matter.

"Youth don't need to care about the Union to join — they join for the concert ticket, and accidentally discover what the Union means."

🤝

02 — Product Ecosystem

How points flow

Youth earn by doing civic actions, accumulate in a national wallet, and redeem for real rewards. Simple to explain. Powerful at scale.

Earn

Civic Actions

Every action that strengthens the Union — education, work, environment, culture — is worth points. VPO defines the menu. Partners verify completion.

07 — Risk & Mitigation

Know the risks. Own the answers.

Every strong idea has risks. Here's what they are and exactly how we handle them.

High — Political
Union politics derail cross-side features
Mitigation: VPO is already the constitutional home of Union coordination. Build the platform under their mandate. Engage Zanzibar government as co-owner, not beneficiary.
High — Adoption
Youth don't sign up or don't stay active
Mitigation: Lead with burudani, not civic content. First reward must be redeemable within 2 weeks of joining. Ambassador programme — 1,000 campus and mtaa youth leaders as evangelists.
Medium — Technology
USSD / mobile infrastructure gaps in rural areas
Mitigation: Phase 1 is urban only. USSD ensures no smartphone dependency. Offline point-earning at physical partner locations (schools, VETA centres, community centres).
Medium — Commercial
Sponsors don't renew after Year 1
Mitigation: Lock 18-month minimum contracts at Dhahabu tier. Share monthly engagement reports proving ROI. First pilot cohort provides proof-of-concept data before renewals are due.
Low — Fraud
Youth game the system for points without real civic action
Mitigation: NIDA-verified identity prevents duplicate accounts. High-value actions (jobs, training) are partner-verified. AI fraud detection in Phase 2.
Low — Privacy
Youth data misuse or breach
Mitigation: Data stays sovereign in Tanzania. VPO is the data custodian. Sponsors get aggregate insights, not individual profiles. Consent is explicit and revocable at any time.
